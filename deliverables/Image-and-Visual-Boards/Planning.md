# Planning

## Purpose

The Image and Visual Boards Form was created to provide participants with a standardized method for submitting visual displays that will be showcased during the Pathways to Possibilities: Launch Your Future Summit. The objective was to collect all information needed to review, organize, and prepare each submission while keeping the process straightforward for participants.

## Planning Process

The planning process began by identifying the information event organizers would need before displaying a visual board. Rather than collecting excessive details, the form was designed to capture only the information necessary to evaluate submissions, prepare display logistics, and communicate with contributors.

The form was organized into logical sections that guide participants through the submission process in the same order event staff will later review the information. This structure reduces confusion for both participants and administrators.

The major planning decisions included:

- Creating a dedicated section for submitter information.
- Separating visual board details from file uploads.
- Including optional display requirement questions to assist with event planning.
- Collecting permission to display submitted work during the summit.
- Maintaining consistency with the layout and wording used throughout the other Pathways to Possibilities forms.

## Branding

After the form structure was completed, the official Pathways to Possibilities event branding was incorporated into the welcome section. The event logo and introductory content were added to create a consistent participant experience across every public-facing form used for the summit.

## Expected Outcome

The completed form provides a simple submission experience while giving event organizers the information necessary to review visual board submissions, prepare display spaces, and communicate with participants before the event.
