# Implementation

## Platform

The registration form was implemented using Submittable as part of the Pathways to Possibilities event management process.

---

## Form Structure

The registration form follows a straightforward workflow designed to guide attendees through the registration process.

Registration Information

↓

Participant Information

↓

Attendance Selection

↓

Accessibility Information

↓

Submission

---

## Fields Implemented

| Field | Type | Required |
|-------|------|----------|
| Full Name | Name | Yes |
| Email Address | Email | Yes |
| Relationship with Empire State University | Dropdown List | Yes |
| Days Attending | Multiple Response | Yes |
| Accessibility Accommodation | Single Response | No |
| Accommodation Details | Long Answer | No |

---

## User Experience

The form was designed to provide a positive user experience by minimizing the number of questions while maintaining the information required by Career Services.

A welcome message introduces the event, communicates the estimated completion time, and explains how registration information will be used.

A privacy notice provides transparency regarding the collection and use of participant information.

The order of questions follows a logical progression that reduces unnecessary navigation and keeps the registration process easy to complete.

---

## Accessibility

Attendees are provided with an opportunity to identify accessibility accommodations prior to the event. Additional details can be supplied through a long-answer field when necessary.

---

## Final Solution

The completed implementation provides Career Services with a standardized registration process that supports attendance planning, participant communication, and accessibility preparation while maintaining a concise registration experience.
