# Decisions

## Purpose

This document explains the design decisions made while building the Student Virtual Poster Session form. Each decision was based on improving the submission experience for students while providing Career Services with the information needed to review and organize poster submissions.

---

## Student-Focused Design

The form was intentionally designed to be shorter and simpler than the Call for Presentations form. Since the audience consists of Empire State University students, the focus shifted away from evaluating the presenter and toward evaluating the academic project itself.

Student information was limited to only what was necessary to identify the submitter and support future communication.

---

## Project-Based Evaluation

The review process is centered on the quality and purpose of the student's work rather than their professional background. For that reason, the form collects information about the poster, project type, and project summary instead of requesting biographies, resumes, or professional experience.

This approach keeps the review process focused on the academic work being presented.

---

## Organized Submission Workflow

The form was divided into sections that follow the natural progression of a submission.

* Student Information
* Poster Information
* Poster Submission
* Presentation Requirements
* Agreements & Permissions

Grouping related questions together creates a more organized experience for students while making completed submissions easier for reviewers to navigate.

---

## Standardized Project Categories

A dropdown list was used for project type to provide consistent reporting across submissions while supporting a wide range of academic experiences, including research, capstone projects, internships, creative works, applied learning projects, and community engagement activities.

Students whose projects do not fit one of the predefined categories can select **Other** and provide additional information.

---

## Flexible File Submission

Poster uploads and supplemental materials were separated into two file upload fields. This allows reviewers to access the primary poster immediately while giving students the option to include supporting documentation when it strengthens their submission.

---

## Minimal Administrative Burden

Throughout development, each field was reviewed to determine whether it served a clear business purpose. Questions that duplicated existing information or did not contribute to the review process were either simplified or removed.

This decision helped keep the form concise without sacrificing the quality of information collected.

---

## Participant Acknowledgments

Rather than presenting lengthy legal statements, the form concludes with three concise acknowledgments confirming originality, permission to display accepted posters, and the accuracy of the submitted information.

This provides the necessary confirmations while maintaining a positive submission experience for students.
