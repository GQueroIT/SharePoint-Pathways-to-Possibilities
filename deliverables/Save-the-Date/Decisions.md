# Decisions

## Purpose

This document records the design decisions made during the development of the **Save the Date Interest Form**. Each decision was made to improve the user experience, support the business objectives of Career Services, and create a foundation for future event communications.

---

## Decision 1 – Introduce the Event Before Collecting Information

The form begins with a welcome message that introduces the **Pathways to Possibilities: Launch Your Future Summit** before requesting any participant information.

### Reason

Providing context first helps users understand the purpose of the event, why they are being asked to complete the form, and how their information will be used. This creates a more welcoming experience than immediately presenting data-entry fields.

---

## Decision 2 – Keep the Form Short

The form was intentionally designed with only the fields necessary to identify interested participants.

### Reason

Requesting too much information during the initial engagement can discourage participation. A shorter form reduces friction, increases completion rates, and reserves detailed questions for the official registration form.

---

## Decision 3 – Require Full Name

A required **Full Name** field was included.

### Reason

Collecting participant names allows Career Services to identify individuals, personalize future communications, and maintain organized submission records.

---

## Decision 4 – Require an Email Address

A required **Email Address** field was included.

### Reason

The primary purpose of the Save the Date Interest Form is to establish a communication list for future event updates. Requiring an email address ensures Career Services can share registration information, schedules, reminders, and event announcements.

---

## Decision 5 – Use a Dropdown for Affiliation

Participant affiliation is collected using predefined options rather than a free-text response.

### Reason

Using a dropdown standardizes responses, improves reporting accuracy, and prevents inconsistent data entry. It also helps Career Services better understand which audiences are expressing interest in the event.

---

## Decision 6 – Confirm Participant Interest

A required confirmation checkbox was included to allow participants to indicate they wish to receive future event communications.

### Reason

This confirms that participants intentionally requested updates about the event and makes the purpose of the form clear before submission.

---

## Decision 7 – Defer Additional Information

Information such as major, graduation year, employer details, accessibility accommodations, and session preferences was intentionally excluded.

### Reason

These questions are more appropriate during the official event registration process. Deferring them keeps the Save the Date Interest Form focused on early engagement and prevents unnecessary data collection.

---

## Decision 8 – Delay Final Branding

Official event branding and graphics were not incorporated during the initial implementation.

### Reason

The form structure was completed first to allow development to continue while waiting for official branding assets from Career Services. Once those assets are provided, they can be incorporated without redesigning the form.

---

## Future Revisions

Following stakeholder review, future updates may include:

- Official Pathways to Possibilities branding.
- Career Services branding.
- Event banner graphics.
- Revised introductory content.
- Additional information requested by stakeholders.
