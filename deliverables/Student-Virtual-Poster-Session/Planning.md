# Planning

## Purpose

The Student Virtual Poster Session form was planned to provide Empire State University students with a simple and organized method for submitting academic posters to the **Pathways to Possibilities: Launch Your Future Summit**. The planning process focused on understanding what information Career Services needed to successfully review submissions while keeping the experience approachable for students.

Unlike the Call for Presentations form, this submission process centers on evaluating the student's project rather than the presenter's professional experience. The goal was to collect meaningful project information without creating an unnecessarily long or complicated submission process.

---

## Business Requirements

The form needed to support several business objectives throughout the review process.

* Identify the student submitting the poster.
* Collect information about the academic project being presented.
* Allow students to upload their completed poster and supporting materials.
* Provide reviewers with enough information to evaluate each submission consistently.
* Collect presentation requirements that may affect participation.
* Obtain the permissions necessary to display accepted posters during the event.

---

## Planning Approach

The form was planned using a user-centered approach that balanced the needs of students, reviewers, and event organizers. Before adding each field, its purpose was evaluated to determine whether the information would be used during the review process or event planning.

Questions that duplicated existing information or did not provide value to reviewers were intentionally excluded. This helped keep the submission process concise while ensuring that all required information could be collected within a single form.

The overall workflow was organized into logical sections so students could move through the submission process naturally without feeling overwhelmed.

---

## Form Organization

The planned structure consisted of five primary sections.

1. Student Information
2. Poster Information
3. Poster Submission
4. Presentation Requirements
5. Agreements & Permissions

Each section was designed to collect related information, making the form easier for students to complete and simpler for reviewers to evaluate.

---

## Expected Outcome

The completed form would provide Career Services with a consistent process for collecting, reviewing, and organizing student poster submissions while creating a positive submission experience for students participating in the Pathways to Possibilities: Launch Your Future Summit.

The planning phase established the foundation for the implementation, ensuring that every field served a clear business purpose before development began.
