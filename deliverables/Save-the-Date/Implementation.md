# Implementation

## Purpose

This document records the implementation process for the **Save the Date Interest Form** created in Submittable for the **Pathways to Possibilities: Launch Your Future Summit**. Each step documents the work completed during development and serves as a reference for recreating the form in the future.

---

## Step 1 – Create the Form

A new form was created within the Submittable workspace to serve as the foundation for the Save the Date Interest Form.

**Result**

The form shell was created and prepared for customization.

**Screenshot**

`01-Form-Created.png`

---

## Step 2 – Configure the Form Title

The default form title was replaced with a descriptive title that clearly identifies the form's purpose.

**Form Title**

> Pathways to Possibilities – Save the Date Interest Form

**Result**

The form can now be easily identified by administrators within the Submittable workspace.

**Screenshot**

`02-Form-Title.png`

---

## Step 3 – Add the Welcome Section

A text-only section was added to the beginning of the form to introduce the event before requesting participant information.

The introduction explains:

- What the event is.
- Why the participant is completing the form.
- What information they can expect to receive after submission.

**Result**

Participants receive context before entering any personal information.

**Screenshot**

`03-Welcome-Section.png`

---

## Step 4 – Add Participant Information Fields

The required participant fields were added to the form.

Fields included:

- Full Name
- Email Address
- Affiliation

Each field was configured to collect only the information necessary during the Save the Date phase.

**Result**

The form captures essential participant information while remaining concise.

**Screenshot**

`04-Participant-Fields.png`

---

## Step 5 – Add Interest Confirmation

A required confirmation checkbox was added to allow participants to indicate that they would like to receive future event communications.

**Result**

Participants explicitly confirm their interest before submitting the form.

**Screenshot**

`05-Interest-Confirmation.png`

---

## Step 6 – Review the Form

After all components were added, the form was reviewed to verify:

- Field order
- Question wording
- Required field settings
- Overall user experience

The completed form was evaluated to ensure it remained simple, informative, and easy to complete.

**Result**

The initial implementation was completed successfully and prepared for stakeholder review.

**Screenshot**

`06-Completed-Form.png`
