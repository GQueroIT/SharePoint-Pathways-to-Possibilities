# Implementation

## Overview

The Student Virtual Poster Session form was implemented in Submittable using a section-based layout that guides students through the submission process from introduction to final submission. The implementation focused on creating a clean and organized experience while ensuring that Career Services receives all of the information needed to review and prepare accepted poster presentations.

---

## Form Development

Implementation began by creating the form structure and establishing the submission name format so that each submission could be easily identified by poster title and student name. A welcome section was added to introduce the Student Virtual Poster Session, explain the purpose of the form, and prepare students for the information they would need before beginning the submission.

The student information section was developed first to collect only the information required to identify the submitter and support future communication. Fields that were not directly related to the review process were intentionally avoided to keep the experience simple.

---

## Poster Information

The poster information section was implemented to collect the details reviewers need to understand each submission. Students provide a poster title, identify the type of academic project being submitted, indicate whether additional student contributors should receive recognition, and complete a project summary describing the purpose, development, and outcomes of their work.

These fields provide reviewers with a consistent overview of every submission while accommodating a variety of academic experiences beyond traditional research projects.

---

## File Submission

Separate file upload fields were implemented for the poster and any supplemental materials. This allows reviewers to immediately access the primary submission while giving students the option to include additional supporting documentation when appropriate.

Organizing uploads into their own section also helps simplify the review process by keeping submission materials separate from descriptive project information.

---

## Presentation Requirements

A dedicated section was implemented to collect any accessibility accommodations or special presentation requirements. Open-ended questions were used instead of predefined checklists, allowing students to communicate individual needs without limiting their responses.

This approach provides flexibility while keeping the form concise.

---

## Agreements and Submission

The final section was implemented using required acknowledgment checkboxes confirming originality, permission to display accepted posters during the event, and the accuracy of the submitted information.

A closing message was added after the acknowledgment section to thank students for their submission and explain that Career Services would contact them if additional information or revisions were needed.

---

## Final Result

The completed implementation provides a structured submission workflow that is easy for students to complete and equally easy for reviewers to navigate. Each section was designed to support a specific stage of the submission process, resulting in a form that balances usability, organization, and the business needs of the Pathways to Possibilities planning team.
