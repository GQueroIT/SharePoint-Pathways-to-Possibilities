# Student Virtual Poster Session

## Overview

The **Student Virtual Poster Session** form was created to provide Empire State University students with a structured way to submit academic posters for the **Pathways to Possibilities: Launch Your Future Summit**. The form supports the review process by collecting the information Career Services needs to evaluate submissions while keeping the experience straightforward and student-friendly.

Unlike the Call for Presentations form, this submission focuses on the student's academic work rather than the presenter's professional background. The form was intentionally designed to collect only the information necessary to review, organize, and prepare accepted posters for the virtual event.

---

## Business Need

Career Services required a standardized submission process that would allow students to submit research projects, capstone work, internships, creative works, community engagement projects, and other academic experiences for consideration during the Student Virtual Poster Session.

The form provides reviewers with consistent project information, allows students to upload poster files and supporting materials, and collects the permissions necessary to display accepted submissions during the summit.

---

## Objectives

* Provide students with a simple and organized submission experience.
* Collect the information needed to evaluate poster submissions.
* Support both individual and collaborative student projects.
* Standardize project information for reviewers.
* Collect poster files and optional supporting materials.
* Gather presentation requirements and participant acknowledgments.

## Lessons Learned

Building this form reinforced the importance of designing around the purpose of the submission rather than following a traditional academic research template. Each field was evaluated to determine whether it supported the review process or the successful organization of the virtual poster session. This approach resulted in a form that remains concise while still collecting the information needed by reviewers and event organizers.

The completed form also demonstrates the value of separating student information, project details, file submissions, presentation requirements, and participant acknowledgments into clearly defined sections, creating a more organized experience for both students and administrators.
