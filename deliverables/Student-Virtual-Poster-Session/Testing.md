# Testing

## Overview

Testing was performed after the Student Virtual Poster Session form was fully implemented in Submittable. The objective was to verify that the form functioned as intended, collected the required information, and provided a logical submission experience for students while meeting the needs of Career Services.

Rather than testing individual fields in isolation, the completed form was reviewed as a complete submission workflow from the perspective of both the student and the event administrators.

---

## Functional Testing

Each section of the form was reviewed to confirm that required fields were identified correctly, optional fields behaved as expected, and field descriptions clearly explained the information being requested.

Dropdown selections, file upload fields, acknowledgment checkboxes, and section organization were also reviewed to ensure that students could progress through the submission without confusion.

---

## Workflow Validation

The overall flow of the form was tested by completing the submission from beginning to end.

Particular attention was given to ensuring that information was requested in a logical order. Student information was collected first, followed by project details, poster uploads, presentation requirements, and participant acknowledgments. This sequence creates a natural progression through the submission process and reduces unnecessary backtracking.

---

## Administrative Review

The completed form was evaluated from the perspective of Career Services to confirm that collected information would support the review process, event planning, and participant communication.

Each field was reviewed to verify that it served a clear business purpose. Duplicate or unnecessary questions were eliminated to keep the form concise while preserving the information needed by reviewers.

---

## User Experience Review

The completed submission process was reviewed with the goal of creating a positive experience for students.

The final form provides clear instructions, organized sections, and concise field descriptions while avoiding unnecessary administrative questions. Students can complete the submission in a relatively short amount of time without sacrificing the quality of information collected.

---

## Testing Outcome

Testing confirmed that the Student Virtual Poster Session form successfully supports the intended submission process for the Pathways to Possibilities: Launch Your Future Summit.

The completed implementation provides Career Services with a consistent method for collecting and reviewing poster submissions while offering students a clear, organized, and user-friendly submission experience.

The form is considered ready for stakeholder review, with any future enhancements to be incorporated following feedback from Career Services and the planning committee.
