# Planning

Before making changes, I reviewed the version of the form that had already been created by the planning committee.

Unlike several of the other forms in this project, this one already contained the committee options and overall structure. Because those items had already been established, I focused on reviewing the form for consistency instead of redesigning it.

My approach was to ask a few simple questions during the review:

- Does the form match the branding used throughout the project?
- Is there enough information for volunteers to understand the purpose of the form?
- Are only the necessary fields being collected?
- Can the form remain short while still meeting the committee's needs?

Since the existing form already met its primary objective, only small improvements were made.
