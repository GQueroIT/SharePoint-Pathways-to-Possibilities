# Planning

## Purpose

The purpose of this deliverable was to create a standardized proposal submission process for individuals interested in presenting at the Pathways to Possibilities: Launch Your Future Summit.

The form needed to collect enough information for the planning committee to evaluate presentation proposals while remaining approachable for presenters with varying levels of speaking experience.

---

## Business Need

Prior to implementation, there was no standardized process for collecting presentation proposals. The planning committee needed a consistent method for gathering presenter information, session details, and proposal summaries that could be reviewed fairly across all submissions.

The solution also needed to reduce administrative follow-up by requesting the information necessary for an initial evaluation without creating an unnecessarily long application.

---

## Planning Objectives

Before building the form, the following objectives were established:

- Create a simple and professional proposal submission experience.
- Organize questions into logical sections to improve usability.
- Collect consistent information for proposal review.
- Balance required information with ease of completion.
- Encourage thoughtful responses through clear prompts and guidance.
- Minimize unnecessary required fields to reduce submission fatigue.

---

## Requirements

The form needed to collect:

- Presenter information
- Organization or affiliation
- Professional background
- Presentation title
- Presentation topic
- Presentation description
- Participant learning outcomes
- Intended audience
- Session format
- Presenter acknowledgement

Optional fields were used where additional context could benefit reviewers without preventing submissions.

---

## Design Approach

The form was planned around the presenter's experience rather than simply listing questions.

Information was grouped into logical sections so presenters could move naturally from introducing themselves to describing their presentation proposal.

This structure was intended to improve readability while making the overall submission process feel shorter and easier to complete.

---

## Expected Outcome

The completed form would provide the event planning committee with consistent proposal information while offering presenters a straightforward and professional submission experience that supports efficient proposal review.
