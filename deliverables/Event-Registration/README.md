# Registration Form

## Pathways to Possibilities: Launch Your Future Summit

---

## Overview

The Registration Form was developed to provide Career Services with a simple, centralized method for registering attendees for the Pathways to Possibilities: Launch Your Future Summit. Rather than relying on email registrations or manually maintained spreadsheets, the form creates a consistent registration experience while collecting only the information necessary to support event planning and communication.

The solution was implemented in Submittable and designed to be completed in approximately one to two minutes. Every question included on the form directly supports an operational need, allowing Career Services to efficiently manage registrations while providing attendees with a straightforward registration process.

---

## Business Problem

Career Services required a standardized method for collecting registrations for a multi-day university event. Without a centralized registration process, tracking attendance, communicating with participants, and preparing for accessibility accommodations becomes more time consuming and increases the likelihood of inconsistent or incomplete information.

The registration process needed to be simple for attendees while providing organizers with the information necessary to prepare for the event.

---

## Solution

A registration form was developed using Submittable to provide a professional, easy-to-use registration experience. The form collects attendee information, planned attendance dates, participant type, and accessibility requests while intentionally avoiding unnecessary questions that would increase completion time without supporting business objectives.

The completed form provides Career Services with accurate registration information while creating a positive user experience for attendees.

---

- Collect only information necessary for event planning.
