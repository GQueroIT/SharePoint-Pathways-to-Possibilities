# Save the Date Interest Form

## Overview

The **Save the Date Interest Form** was created to support the early planning phase of the **Pathways to Possibilities: Launch Your Future Summit**. The purpose of the form is to identify individuals who would like to receive updates as additional event information becomes available.

This form is the first point of engagement with prospective attendees and was intentionally designed to be brief, informative, and easy to complete.

---

## Business Need

Career Services required a method to notify students, alumni, faculty, employers, and community partners about the upcoming event before registration officially opened.

The form allows interested participants to express interest while providing Career Services with a reliable list of individuals who wish to receive future communications regarding the event.

---

## Objectives

- Introduce the Pathways to Possibilities event.
- Generate early awareness.
- Capture contact information for future communications.
- Keep the submission process simple and user-friendly.
- Establish a foundation for future event registration.

## Lessons Learned

Designing the form reinforced the importance of providing users with context before requesting personal information. Beginning the form with a brief introduction helps participants understand the purpose of the event and why their information is being collected, resulting in a clearer and more welcoming user experience.

Future revisions will incorporate official branding and any feedback provided by Career Services.
