# Committee Volunteer Form

## Overview

The Committee Volunteer Form was created to collect volunteer information for individuals interested in serving on one of the planning committees supporting the Pathways to Possibilities event.

Unlike the public-facing registration and submission forms, this form was designed as an internal administrative tool. My goal during implementation was not to redesign the volunteer process, but to review the existing committee-approved form, apply consistent branding, improve the presentation, and make the experience consistent with the other forms developed for the project.

---

## What I Changed

After reviewing the original form provided by the committee, I made a few improvements while intentionally keeping the overall structure intact.

The updates included:

- Adding the approved Pathways to Possibilities logo.
- Adding a brief welcome message to explain the purpose of the form.
- Creating a meaningful Submission Name field for easier identification inside Submittable.
- Adding an Email Address field so committee members have a reliable way to contact volunteers.
- Updating the committee selection question so it reads more naturally.
- Preserving the committee names supplied by the planning committee.

The objective was to enhance the form without changing the committee-approved workflow.

---

## Result

The completed form matches the branding and layout of the other Pathways to Possibilities forms while remaining simple and easy to complete.
