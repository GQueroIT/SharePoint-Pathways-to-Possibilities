# Deliverable 03 – Call for Presentations

## Overview

The Call for Presentations form was developed to support the Pathways to Possibilities: Launch Your Future Summit by providing a structured submission process for individuals interested in presenting during the event.

The form collects presenter information, presentation details, and session information while maintaining a submission experience that is simple, organized, and accessible. Information gathered through the form allows the event planning committee to review proposals consistently and identify presentations that align with the goals of the summit.

---

## Business Need

The summit required a centralized process for collecting presentation proposals from a diverse group of presenters, including:

- SUNY Empire students
- Faculty
- Staff
- Alumni
- Employers
- Career professionals
- Community partners

Without a standardized submission process, proposal reviews would be inconsistent and administrative follow-up would increase significantly.

---

## Objectives

- Standardize presentation proposal submissions
- Collect consistent information for proposal review
- Reduce administrative communication
- Improve proposal quality through guided prompts
- Create an accessible and professional submission experience
- Support efficient review by the event planning committee

---

## Solution

A multi-section proposal form was created in Submittable using a combination of text fields, dropdown menus, and long-answer responses.

The form guides presenters through the submission process by collecting:

- Presenter information
- Professional background
- Presentation proposal
- Session details
- Presenter acknowledgement

Information is organized into logical sections to improve readability while minimizing unnecessary questions.

---

## Design Considerations

Several design decisions were made during development:

- Keep the proposal concise while collecting meaningful information.
- Reduce unnecessary required fields.
- Allow first-time presenters to apply without disadvantage.
- Encourage thoughtful responses through descriptive prompts instead of lengthy applications.
- Separate presenter information from proposal information to improve user experience.

---

## Key Features

- Guided proposal workflow
- Presenter profile collection
- Topic categorization
- Presentation summary
- Participant learning outcomes
- Audience selection
- Session format selection
- Presenter agreement
- Submission naming convention using Presentation Title – Presenter Name
