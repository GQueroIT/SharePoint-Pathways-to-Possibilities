# Implementation

I began by reviewing the existing Committee Volunteer Form that had been provided by the planning committee.

The first change was adding the approved Pathways to Possibilities logo so the form would immediately match the branding used throughout the rest of the project.

Next, I added a short welcome message explaining the purpose of the form and letting volunteers know their responses would be used to coordinate committee assignments and future communication.

I enabled the Submission Name field and configured it as:

Volunteer Name - Committee Preference

This makes each submission easier to identify inside the Submittable dashboard.

The existing Volunteer Name field was retained, and I added an Email Address field so volunteers could be contacted after submitting the form.

The committee selection question was reviewed for clarity, but the committee options themselves were left unchanged because they had already been established by the planning committee.

Once the updates were complete, I reviewed the form to verify that the branding, field order, and overall presentation were consistent with the other Pathways to Possibilities forms.
