# Planning

## Purpose

The purpose of this document is to capture the planning process for the **Save the Date Interest Form** developed for the **Pathways to Possibilities: Launch Your Future Summit**. This document records the business need, design objectives, and planning decisions that guided the development of the form before implementation.

---

## Business Need

Career Services needed a way to begin engaging potential participants before event registration officially opened. The goal was to create a simple form that allowed interested individuals to express interest and receive future communications as additional event details became available.

The form serves as the first point of contact between the event organizers and prospective attendees.

---

## Project Goals

The Save the Date Interest Form was designed to:

- Introduce the upcoming event.
- Generate awareness.
- Build an early contact list.
- Collect only the information necessary at this stage.
- Provide a positive and welcoming user experience.

---

## Design Objectives

Several design principles guided the planning process.

### Keep the form simple.

The form should be quick to complete and avoid asking for unnecessary information before registration opens.

### Provide context before requesting information.

Participants should understand what the event is, why they are being asked to complete the form, and how their information will be used before entering any personal information.

### Collect only essential data.

Only information needed to communicate with interested participants should be collected during this phase of the project.

---

## Information to Collect

The planned fields included:

- Full Name
- Email Address
- Affiliation
- Interest Confirmation

Additional participant information would be deferred to the official event registration form.

---

## Assumptions

During planning, several assumptions were made.

- Official event branding would be provided at a later date.
- Additional event details would become available before registration opened.
- Stakeholder feedback may require revisions before the form is finalized.

These assumptions allowed development to continue without delaying implementation.

---

## Success Criteria

The form would be considered successful if it:

- Clearly introduced the event.
- Was easy to understand.
- Could be completed in only a few minutes.
- Collected accurate contact information.
- Created a foundation for future event communications.

---

## Next Phase

After planning was completed, the form was implemented in Submittable, documented, tested, and prepared for stakeholder review.
