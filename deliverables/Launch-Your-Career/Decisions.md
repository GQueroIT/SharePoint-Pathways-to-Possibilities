# Design Decisions

## Branding

I added the approved Pathways to Possibilities logo so this form would have the same appearance as the other Submittable forms created for the event.

I also added a short welcome message explaining why the form exists and how the information will be used.

---

## Committee Names

The committee names were provided by the planning committee.

Rather than renaming or reorganizing them, I chose to leave them as they were. Since these names represent the committee's organizational structure, preserving their wording was more appropriate than introducing my own changes.

Only minor wording improvements for readability were considered.

---

## Submission Name

I configured the Submission Name field as:

Volunteer Name - Committee Preference

This provides a meaningful identifier when reviewing submissions inside Submittable.

---

## Form Length

One of my goals throughout this project has been keeping forms as simple as possible.

For this deliverable, I decided not to introduce additional questions about experience, availability, or volunteer interests because those requirements had not been requested by the committee. The form collects only the information needed to identify the volunteer and their committee preference.
