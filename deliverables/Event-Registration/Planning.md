# Planning

## Business Need

Career Services needed a reliable method for collecting attendee registrations for the Pathways to Possibilities: Launch Your Future Summit. The registration process needed to support planning for a multi-day event while remaining simple enough that attendees could complete it quickly.

The primary goal was to create a registration experience that minimized unnecessary questions while collecting the information required to communicate with attendees, estimate attendance, and prepare accessibility accommodations.

---

## Stakeholders

- Empire State University Career Services
- Current Students
- Alumni
- Faculty and Staff
- Employers and Community Partners
- Prospective Students
- Event Planning Committee

---

## Functional Requirements

The registration form must:

- Collect attendee name.
- Collect an email address.
- Identify the participant's relationship with the university.
- Allow attendees to select which days they plan to attend.
- Capture accessibility accommodation requests.
- Validate required information before submission.

---

## Non-Functional Requirements

The registration form should:

- Be easy to complete.
- Require minimal time.
- Present information in a logical order.
- Support desktop and mobile devices.
- Maintain a professional appearance.
- Protect participant information.

---

## Assumptions

- Most attendees will complete the registration online.
- Email will serve as the primary communication method.
- Accessibility requests will be reviewed manually.
- Registration should take approximately one to two minutes.

---

## Success Criteria

The implementation is considered successful when:

- Users can successfully submit registrations.
- Required information is collected.
- Accessibility requests are captured.
- Career Services can review registration information efficiently.
- The registration process remains simple and user friendly.
