## Usability Review

The registration form was reviewed to ensure that:

- Instructions were clear.
- Questions followed a logical order.
- Completion time remained approximately one to two minutes.
- Required fields were clearly identified.
- The overall layout remained clean and professional.

---

## Results

Testing confirmed that the registration form successfully captures the information required for event planning while maintaining a simple registration experience.

The implementation met all planned functional requirements and is suitable for production use.
